# Article Catalog — bengkel.co.id

## Scope

This catalog plans an Indonesian workshop-service knowledge layer for evidenced welding/fabrication, selected industrial machine/component repair, structural metalwork, pipe/tank/cylinder work, and routed fabricated products. It emphasizes customer intake, inspection, decisions, procurement, execution evidence, QA, safety, maintenance, aftercare, disputes, environment, and provider accountability.

Automotive, general electrical installation, HVAC, electronics, plumbing, general building renovation, and non-metal furniture are not technical catalog scopes. Existing promotional mentions remain capability questions pending real routes, staff/tool evidence, procedures, cases, and qualified reviewers. `bengkel-las.co.id` may cover the same welding queries independently; only collisions within `bengkel.co.id` are controlled here.

## First-wave standard

The cluster links scope → intake → inspection → intervention decision → quote → ITP/safety → handover → capability proof.

Measure sitemap discovery, indexation, selected canonicals, impressions grouped by primary intent, checklist completion/downloads, useful internal-link journeys, qualified enquiries containing better scope evidence, enquiry response rate, and page/query pairs that suggest cannibalization. Do not use ranking as the only success signal.

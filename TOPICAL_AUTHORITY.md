# Topical Authority — bengkel.co.id

## Role and boundary

`bengkel.co.id` should become an Indonesian buyer-and-operator reference for commissioning workshop work: identifying a need, reporting symptoms, arranging inspection, comparing a repair with replacement or fabrication, controlling scope and evidence, accepting the result, maintaining it, and resolving problems. The supported technical systems are the ones evidenced by real routes and articles in this repository: welded and fabricated metalwork, selected industrial machines and components, structural metalwork, pipework, tanks and cylinders, and fabricated residential products. The audience includes homeowners, facility and maintenance teams, small manufacturers, procurement staff, project supervisors, and workshop operators. The geographic frame is Indonesia, with humidity, rain, coastal corrosion, informal procurement, site access, and current Indonesian K3 and consumer-protection context treated where relevant.

The site is **not presently evidenced as an automotive authority**. Automotive, electrical installation, HVAC, electronics, plumbing, building renovation, and general furniture appear only as homepage/about-page promotional cards or images. They have no service route, technical article, case evidence, staff qualification, equipment inventory, inspection method, warranty scope, or handover record in this repository. Their capability status is therefore unresolved rather than assumed. Technical briefs for those systems are excluded until the business proves the offer and assigns qualified reviewers. The same gate applies to make/model-specific vehicle advice: use the applicable OEM workshop information and a qualified automotive reviewer, not generic instructions.

Existing `/las` routes own service and quotation conversion. Educational articles may help readers prepare for those services but cannot imply an unsupported capability, certify structural fitness, authorize hot work, select electrical protection, approve pressure equipment, or replace a competent inspection. `bengkel-las.co.id` may independently cover welding and fabrication in greater process depth. Cross-domain overlap is allowed; the intent boundaries in this repository control only pages on `bengkel.co.id`.

Current primary-source gates checked on 2026-07-23:

- [UU No. 1 Tahun 1970](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/47614/uu-no-1-tahun-1970) remains in force as the general Indonesian workplace-safety law.
- [Permenaker No. 11 Tahun 2026](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/351282/permenaker-no-11-tahun-2026) took effect on 2026-07-03 and changes labor-inspection procedure while partially revoking specified provisions in several K3 regulations. Article research must check the consolidated current status rather than cite an old instrument in isolation.
- [Permenaker No. 5 Tahun 2018](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/1546/peraturan-menteri-nomor-5-tahun-2018), [Permenaker No. 12 Tahun 2015](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/610/peraturan-menteri-nomor-12-tahun-2015), [Permenaker No. 37 Tahun 2016](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/146211/permenaker-no-37-tahun-2016), and [Permenaker No. 38 Tahun 2016](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/146207/permenaker-no-38-tahun-2016) remain relevant to workplace environment, electrical K3, pressure vessels/tanks, and powered/production machinery, but each publication must verify amendments and partial revocations.
- [Permenaker No. 11 Tahun 2023](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/282464) governs confined-space K3 and is especially relevant to tank entry; specified provisions were partially revoked in 2026.
- BSN lists [SNI 1729:2020](https://pesta.bsn.go.id/produk/detail/12882-sni17292020) as a current structural-steel building specification. It is not a universal design recipe, and project applicability still needs a structural engineer.
- ISO records [ISO 3834-6:2024](https://www.iso.org/standard/83335.html) for implementing fusion-welding quality requirements, [ISO 5817:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/80209.html) for weld-imperfection quality levels, and [ISO 17635:2025](https://www.iso.org/standard/85705.html) for NDT method selection and evaluation. Contractual adoption, applicable parts, acceptance level, personnel competence, and project code must be verified.
- OSHA's primary technical references for [welding and cutting](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.252) and [hazard communication/SDS](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200) are useful secondary jurisdictional guidance, not Indonesian law.
- [UU No. 8 Tahun 1999](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Home/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999.8Presiden) remains in force for consumer protection; legal interpretations and dispute strategy require qualified Indonesian review.
- [PP No. 22 Tahun 2021](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/161852/pp-no-22-tahun-2021) remains in force and covers environmental approvals plus B3 and non-B3 waste management. Exact obligations depend on the activity, scale, materials, and local implementation.

## Existing coverage and risks

The domain name and broad homepage promise create a useful workshop-service umbrella, but the actual source is a welding/fabrication microsite surrounded by unsupported category cards. Five risks follow:

1. Readers can mistake promotional breadth for verified competence, especially for vehicles, electrical systems, HVAC, or electronics.
2. Existing service articles mix education and sales intent, use absolute quality/safety claims, and do not show the measurements, qualifications, procedures, inspection records, or project evidence needed to substantiate them.
3. `/las/tangki-silinder`, `/las/pipa`, special structures, and industrial repair touch pressure, confined-space, structural, rotating-equipment, electrical, fire, gas, and chemical hazards without clear professional stop conditions.
4. `/las` and several root posts compete for broad welding/fabrication intent; every future article needs a narrower diagnostic, decision, process, or evidence job.
5. Static-export artifacts, two sitemap indexes, archive pages, a legacy `/?p=752` link, inconsistent contact email copy, and unverified superlatives weaken trust and crawl clarity.

## Internal-link architecture

- `/berita` becomes the editorial directory and links to all 20 hubs. Every article links upward to its topic hub; every hub links to its six children.
- Buyer lifecycle: BKL-01 → BKL-02 → BKL-03 → BKL-04 → BKL-05 → supported system BKL-08–BKL-12 → BKL-14 → BKL-17 → BKL-16.
- Safety is embedded, not hidden: every procedural, site, machine, structure, pipe/tank, or chemical page links to the specific BKL-15 control and states prerequisites and stop conditions in the body.
- Diagnostics link to the next decision: BKL-02 symptom pages → BKL-03 inspection → BKL-04 repair/replace → BKL-14 verification → BKL-16 prevention.
- Commercial links are selective. Educational articles link to `/las`, a product child route, or `/kontak-kami` only after answering the reader's task and only when that route's capability is substantiated.
- BKL-10 structural and BKL-11 pressure/confined-space pages always link to their engineering, inspection, and authorization gates; no path routes around qualified review.
- BKL-17 warranty pages link to BKL-18 only for unresolved problems; BKL-18 links back to BKL-03/BKL-14 for evidence and BKL-17 for the promised terms.
- BKL-19 connects materials in BKL-06, methods in BKL-08, maintenance in BKL-16, and vendor evidence in BKL-20 without claiming that repair is always the greener choice.
- Related IDs in `ARTICLE_CATALOG.md` provide per-article lateral links; no generic link block is copied sitewide and no planned article is orphaned.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Label claims as repository observation, stable explanation, manufacturer/OEM requirement, project assumption, current law/standard, test result, or editorial recommendation.
- Recheck BPK/JDIH, BSN/SNI, ISO, manufacturer/OEM documentation, and local requirements on publication day. Permenaker 11/2026 makes stale copied regulation summaries particularly risky.
- Vehicle, structural, electrical, hot-work, gas/pressure, confined-space, chemical, lifting, fire-damaged, and rotating-machinery claims require current primary sources and named qualified review appropriate to the asset and jurisdiction.
- Never invent a qualification, certification, license, service area, partner, price, acceptance criterion, tolerance, machine setting, test result, case study, project photo, warranty, or response time.
- Technical procedures state prerequisites, responsible role, isolation, hazards, stop conditions, inspection/measurement method, acceptance source, and records. PPE never replaces source or engineering control.
- For automotive content, require a proved service route and equipment/competence inventory, then use the exact make/model/year OEM workshop information and a qualified automotive reviewer. Until then, only transparent referral/scope content is allowed.
- Structural content cannot provide project sizing or repair details without an engineer. Cite the applicable SNI/project code and distinguish design, fabrication, erection, inspection, and owner approvals.
- Electrical content is limited to hazard recognition and competent-person boundaries unless licensed scope is evidenced. Never publish energized-work or generic vehicle-electrical repair instructions.
- Hot-work and gas content requires current site risk assessment, permit responsibility, combustible control, fire watch where applicable, cylinder/equipment controls, ventilation, emergency plan, and qualified K3 review.
- Chemical and coating content uses current SDS, process-specific exposure controls, labeling/storage requirements, spill response, and lawful waste routing. Odor is not treated as an exposure monitor.
- NDT pages distinguish indication, evaluation, and acceptance. Method selection, personnel competence, calibration, coverage, and acceptance criteria come from the governing project requirements.
- Calculators show formulas, units, uncertainty, assumptions, input limits, and professional-review triggers. Cases use real, consented records and clearly separate before condition, intervention, verification, and outcome.
- Consumer and dispute content is neutral education, cites current primary law, preserves evidence, and receives qualified Indonesian legal review before publication.
- Business claims use auditable proof: legal identity, staff competence, equipment limits, current calibration, material certificates, procedures, inspection records, original project evidence, explicit warranty terms, and measured response data.

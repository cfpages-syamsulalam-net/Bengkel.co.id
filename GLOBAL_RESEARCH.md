## Purpose

This is the reusable pre-writing evidence layer for the complete `bengkel.co.id` catalog. It supports an Indonesian buyer-and-operator reference for commissioning workshop work: welded and fabricated metalwork, selected industrial machinery and components, structural metalwork, pipework, tanks and cylinders, and fabricated residential products.

It is not an article, a quotation, a method statement, an engineering design, a legal opinion, a substitute for licensed standards, or evidence that `bengkel.co.id` or any named workshop has a capability, qualification, approval, service area, price, warranty, or safety system. Article writers must open and cite the original sources, not cite this file as authority.

## How writers must use this file

1. Start with the coverage row for the relevant `BKL-*` parent topic.
2. Open every mapped original artifact before using a technical, legal, safety, product, performance, or compliance claim.
3. Treat an official catalog page or public standards abstract as evidence of document identity, status, and visible scope only. Obtain the licensed full text for requirements, tables, tolerances, acceptance levels, and procedures.
4. Distinguish an observation from a diagnosis; a repair proposal from an approved design; workmanship from complete-system fitness; and a workshop's statement from independently verified evidence.
5. Apply Indonesian law first. Foreign government material in this file is conceptual guidance only and never an Indonesian legal requirement.
6. Preserve every stop condition, exclusion, applicability boundary, and professional-review gate.
7. Recheck all legal and standards status when an outline begins, whenever the asset/site/jurisdiction changes, or six months after the verification date above, whichever comes first.

### KR-03 — Customer intake, symptoms, hazardous energy, and stop conditions

- **Sources:** [Permenaker No. 38 Tahun 2016 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/146207/permenaker-no-38-tahun-2016), [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 — control of hazardous energy](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.147), [Permenaker No. 11 Tahun 2026 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/351282/permenaker-no-11-tahun-2026).
- **Grade:** A-ID for Indonesian records; D-GOV for the US energy-control source.
- **Purpose:** Make intake articles collect decision-relevant evidence without encouraging an unqualified customer or writer to energize, dismantle, enter, pressurize, or test an unsafe asset.
- **Summary:** Useful intake separates the reported symptom, operating state, recent changes, prior intervention, environment, consequence, and available records. Inspection or servicing may expose mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, gravitational, chemical, or stored-pressure energy.
- **Grounded facts:** OSHA's US rule illustrates an energy-control program that distinguishes normal production from servicing and requires isolation/control before relevant servicing; it has explicit scope and exceptions. - **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-02`, `BKL-03`, `BKL-09`, `BKL-13`, and `BKL-16`. Build intake checklists around identity, symptom chronology, operational state, energy sources, access, drawings/manuals, photographs, prior repairs, and an explicit “do not operate or disturb” escalation path.

### KR-04 — Survey, measurement, diagnosis, and inspection-plan discipline

- **Sources:** [ISO 17635:2025 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/85705.html), [ISO 9712:2021 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/75614.html).
- **Grade:** C-STD.
- **Purpose:** Prevent visual symptoms or an isolated measurement from being presented as a complete diagnosis or acceptance decision.
- **Summary:** ISO 17635 frames NDT method selection for metallic welds around quality requirements, material, thickness, welding process, and extent of testing. ISO 9712 addresses qualification and certification of NDT personnel across defined methods.
- **Grounded facts:** Inspection method, coverage, technique, personnel, equipment, surface condition, calibration/verification status, reporting, and acceptance basis are separate evidence items. ISO 17635 also warns that NDT acceptance levels are not a direct one-to-one translation of ISO 5817 quality levels.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-03`, `BKL-09`–`BKL-12`, `BKL-14`, and `BKL-16`. Structure diagnosis as “reported symptom → safe observation → measurement/test → interpretation → uncertainty → intervention decision,” with hold points for specialist testing.
- **Limits/recheck:** The abstracts do not supply technique parameters, acceptance values, required extent, calibration intervals, or project fitness. Obtain the governing code, full standards, written procedure, and asset-specific inspection plan.

### KR-05 — Repair, reinforcement, remanufacture, replacement, monitoring, or defer

- **Sources:** [ISO 15614-1:2017 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/51792.html), [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.212 — general machine guarding](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.212).
- **Grade:** C-STD for the welding-procedure scope; D-GOV for the US guarding source; Gate for the intervention decision.
- **Purpose:** Replace simplistic “can it be welded?” answers with an evidence-led decision that accounts for function, failure consequence, material, geometry, damage mechanism, interfaces, and verification.
- **Summary:** ISO 15614-1 expressly includes production, repair, and build-up welding within its stated material/process scope, but qualifies a proposed welding procedure through tests. The machine-guarding source illustrates that a repaired component must be considered within the operating machine and danger-zone system.
- **Grounded facts:** A technically executable weld is not automatically an acceptable repair. A decision may require material identity, crack/damage mechanism, remaining section, loading and duty, distortion risk, heat-treatment history, OEM restrictions, code rules, access, proof/testing plan, and the consequences of failure.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-04`, `BKL-09`–`BKL-12`, and `BKL-16`. Present alternatives and the evidence each needs; require escalation for load-bearing, rotating, lifting, pressure-retaining, safety-critical, fatigue-sensitive, or unknown-material parts.
- **Limits/recheck:** No generic article may approve a repair design, remaining life, post-repair rating, or continued operation. Those require the asset owner, OEM where applicable, governing code, qualified designer/engineer, and documented verification.

### KR-09 — Welding procedure and welder qualification are different evidence

- **Sources:** [ISO 15614-1:2017 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/51792.html), [ISO 9606-1:2012 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/54936.html), [ISO 3834-6:2024 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/83335.html).
- **Grade:** C-STD.
- **Purpose:** Prevent an article from treating a welder card, a WPS, a procedure test, workmanship, and the finished joint's acceptance as interchangeable.
- **Summary:** ISO 15614-1 qualifies a preliminary welding procedure through a procedure test for its defined processes/materials and ranges. ISO 9606-1 tests a welder's manual manipulation ability for fusion welding of steels and excludes fully mechanized and automated processes. ISO 3834-6 concerns implementation of welding quality requirements.
- **Grounded facts:** Procedure qualification, production WPS, welder/operator qualification, material/process/position/thickness/diameter range, continuity/validity, equipment condition, consumable control, preheat/interpass control, inspection, and final acceptance are distinct records.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-08`, `BKL-09`–`BKL-12`, `BKL-14`, and `BKL-20`. Ask whether the documents cover the actual process, material group, geometry, range, essential variables, examiner/certification route, validity, and project code.
- **Limits/recheck:** Both welding-qualification standards are current at verification but under revision. The public abstracts do not provide qualification ranges or test requirements; recheck editions and use licensed text plus the project specification.

### KR-17 — Preventive maintenance, reliability, downtime, and release to service

- **Sources:** [Permenaker No. 38 Tahun 2016 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/146207/permenaker-no-38-tahun-2016), [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 — control of hazardous energy](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.147), [Permenaker No. 11 Tahun 2026 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/351282/permenaker-no-11-tahun-2026).
- **Grade:** A-ID for Indonesian context; D-GOV for isolation concepts; Gate for asset tasks and intervals.
- **Purpose:** Make maintenance content evidence-based without inventing universal schedules or promising uptime.
- **Summary:** Useful maintenance begins with asset criticality, failure modes, duty/environment, manufacturer and statutory tasks, observed condition, parts/skills/access, safe isolation, verification, and a controlled return to service.
- **Grounded facts:** Calendar-based, runtime-based, condition-based, corrective, and statutory work answer different needs. Work order history, failure coding, inspection readings, parts identity, deferred defects, tests, and release authority support trend review and planning.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-16`, `BKL-02`–`BKL-04`, `BKL-09`, `BKL-14`, and `BKL-17`. Describe how to build an asset-specific program and critical-spares/downtime decision, while preserving OEM, legal, warranty, and safety gates.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not provide a universal interval, alarm threshold, remaining-life estimate, spares quantity, uptime saving, or restart decision. Obtain current OEM manuals, asset history, operating context, statutory requirements, and responsible-person approval.

### KR-18 — Handover, warranty, records, and aftercare

- **Sources:** [UU No. 8 Tahun 1999 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999.8Presiden), [ISO 3834-6:2024 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/83335.html).
- **Grade:** A-ID for consumer context; C-STD for welding-quality implementation scope; Gate for contract terms.
- **Purpose:** Define handover as an evidence package and accepted status, not merely physical delivery or payment.
- **Summary:** Depending on the project, handover may connect approved scope/revisions, drawings, material and part traceability, procedure/personnel records, inspection/test reports, nonconformance closeout, coating records, operating/maintenance instructions, exclusions, warranty terms, photographs, and acceptance.
- **Grounded facts:** Warranty scope, duration, conditions, exclusions, response path, and evidence responsibilities should be explicit. A certificate or checklist is meaningful only if it identifies the item, revision, issuer, date, basis, result, and authorization.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-17`, `BKL-05`–`BKL-12`, `BKL-14`, `BKL-16`, `BKL-18`, and `BKL-20`. Match the handover index to the approved scope and risk; retain unresolved items and responsible parties visibly.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not invent a warranty or imply that a generic dossier proves compliance or fitness. Contract requirements, responsible-party approvals, and actual records govern.

### KR-19 — Complaints, disputes, consumer rights, and remedy evidence

- **Sources:** [UU No. 8 Tahun 1999 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999.8Presiden).
- **Grade:** A-ID for legal context; Gate for legal interpretation and remedy.
- **Purpose:** Help a reader document and triage a problem without prejudging fault, destroying evidence, or giving legal advice.
- **Summary:** A useful complaint record preserves contract/scope and revisions, communications, item identity, dates, symptoms, safe operating status, photographs, measurements/test reports, prior interventions, loss/impact, requested action, access, and evidence custody.
- **Grounded facts:** Immediate safety containment, technical cause investigation, contractual responsibility, consumer remedy, and legal process are different questions. Destructive repair or continued operation can change the condition and evidence.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-18`, `BKL-02`–`BKL-05`, `BKL-14`, `BKL-17`, and `BKL-20`. Add a stop-use/escalation path for safety-critical symptoms, a neutral chronology, joint-inspection options, and routes to independent technical or legal advice.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not state who is liable, promise a refund/rework outcome, calculate damages, or prescribe a limitation period. Check current law, contract, forum, evidence, and competent Indonesian advice.

### KR-20 — Waste, scrap, emissions, noise, chemicals, and circularity

- **Sources:** [PP No. 22 Tahun 2021 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/161852/pp-no-22-tahun-2021), [Permenaker No. 5 Tahun 2018 — official Kemnaker JDIH](https://jdih.kemnaker.go.id/peraturan/detail/1546/peraturan-menteri-nomor-5-tahun-2018), [OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1200 — hazard communication](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1200).
- **Grade:** A-ID for Indonesian environment/workplace context; D-GOV for US communication concepts; Gate for classification and disposal.
- **Purpose:** Connect source reduction, controlled use, segregation, reuse/recycling, worker exposure, and legal waste disposition without assuming every metal offcut is harmless or recyclable.
- **Summary:** PP 22/2021 covers environmental approvals and protection/management of water, air, sea, B3 and non-B3 waste, supervision, and sanctions. Workshop streams can include clean metal, mixed/contaminated scrap, spent abrasives, dust, slag, oils, solvents, coatings, containers, wastewater, fumes, and noise.
- **Grounded facts:** Waste identity, contamination, generator/site context, storage, labeling, transport, receiver authorization, records, and current local rules determine the route. Reuse and repair may avoid new material but do not automatically reduce total environmental or safety impact.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-19`, `BKL-06`, `BKL-08`, `BKL-13`, `BKL-15`, `BKL-16`, and `BKL-20`. Apply the hierarchy of avoiding/reducing waste, suitable reuse, segregated recovery, and authorized disposition, supported by exact product and waste evidence.
- **Limits/recheck:** Do not classify a waste, recommend mixing, state a storage limit, name a legal carrier/receiver, or claim a quantified environmental benefit without current Indonesian/local requirements, characterization, mass/energy data, and authorized-party evidence.

### KR-21 — Vendor capability, business evidence, and accountable claims

- **Sources:** [ISO 3834-6:2024 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/83335.html), [ISO 9606-1:2012 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/54936.html), [ISO 9712:2021 — official ISO abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/75614.html), [UU No. 8 Tahun 1999 — BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/45288/uu-no-8-tahun-1999.8Presiden).
- **Grade:** C-STD for systems/personnel scope; A-ID for consumer context; Gate for every vendor-specific claim.
- **Purpose:** Turn vague claims such as “professional,” “certified,” “experienced,” or “SNI” into verifiable, scope-matched evidence.
- **Summary:** Capability evidence is layered: legal/business identity, responsible contacts, similar-work records, personnel qualifications, procedures, equipment and maintenance/calibration, material control, HSE system, inspection/testing access, subcontractor control, capacity/schedule, insurance/contract evidence where relevant, and traceable handover.
- **Grounded facts:** A person certificate has a defined method/process/range and validity; it does not certify the whole company or project. A quality-system claim does not prove a particular design, welder, NDT technician, machine, material, joint, result, or statutory approval.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `BKL-20`, `BKL-05`, `BKL-08`–`BKL-18`. Ask for document issuer, identifier, scope, revision/validity, holder, applicability to the actual work, and an independent verification route; record gaps and subcontracted responsibilities.
- **Limits/recheck:** No vendor or `bengkel.co.id` capability is evidenced here. Do not publish badges, client names, project photos, certifications, addresses, response times, or warranties without current attributable primary evidence and permission.

## Source-status and volatility notes

- `Permenaker No. 11 Tahun 2026` is the controlling current-status checkpoint for this research. It took effect on 3 July 2026 and partially revoked provisions across several older K3 regulations. Writers must not quote an older regulation without checking the consolidated effect.
- `ISO 15614-1:2017` and `ISO 9606-1:2012` were published/current at verification but ISO marks both as expected to be revised. Recheck edition status before an outline relies on them.
- `ISO 12944-5:2019` is published but ISO marks it “to be revised.” Use the current selected paint manufacturer's documents and the governing project specification in addition to the applicable full standards.
- The OSHA sources are United States regulations. They are retained only as direct government explanations of energy-control, guarding, hazard-communication, and hot-work concepts; they create no Indonesian compliance claim.
- BSN and ISO public pages do not expose the complete normative text. Their presence in this register never authorizes copying hidden tables or inventing requirements.

## Pre-publication stop rules

Stop the outline or article and obtain additional evidence if any of the following is true:

1. The article would diagnose a live asset, approve continued operation, or prescribe disassembly without `GATE-02` and `GATE-08`.
2. It would state a dimension, load, capacity, connection, tolerance, inspection extent, acceptance value, test pressure, or repair procedure without `GATE-04` and any applicable specialist gate.
3. It would cover structural, pressure-retaining, rotating, lifting, fatigue-sensitive, overhead, or other safety-critical work without named professional responsibility under `GATE-05`.
4. It would publish welding variables or qualification claims without `GATE-06`.
5. It would call an indication acceptable/rejectable or a result calibrated/certified without `GATE-07`.
6. It would describe hot work, entry, isolation, electrical work, lifting, or emergency arrangements as safe without the current task/site evidence in `GATE-08` and, where relevant, `GATE-09`.
7. It would name a price, warranty, service capability, certification, customer/project, location, response time, or provider result without `GATE-10` and `GATE-13`.
8. It would promise a legal remedy, assign blame, or classify/dispose of waste without `GATE-11` or `GATE-12`.

When a gate cannot be resolved, the safe article pattern is to explain the decision inputs, evidence to request, questions to ask, warning signs, stop conditions, and the qualified party who must decide—without filling the gap with a rule of thumb.
